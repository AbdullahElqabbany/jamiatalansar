Programs & Services

Everything our community offers

Afternoon Madrasah

Quran and Islamic studies classes held after school, helping children build a strong foundation in faith and recitation.

Learn more →
Youth Programs

Engaging Islamic education, sports, and mentorship designed to keep our young people connected to faith and community.

Learn more →
Family Night

A monthly evening of food, fellowship, and reflection — bringing families together to strengthen bonds within our community.

Learn more →
Daily Gatherings

All five daily prayers held in congregation, with regular halaqas and reminders to nourish the soul throughout the week.

Learn more →
Community Services

Food bank support, newcomer assistance, and marriage services — practical help rooted in compassion for our neighbours.

Learn more →
Sisters' Monthly Program

A dedicated space for sisters to gather for classes, discussions, and social events that build sisterhood and faith.

Learn more →
Foundational Islamic Program

A structured program offering a balanced foundation in the Islamic sciences through traditional study and practical application.

Learn more →
Here We Belong

Here We Belong helps people recognize and respond to Islamophobia, offering reporting, support, and community resources for youth, women, seniors, families, and educators.
