Anger is a natural human emotion, but when left unchecked, it can become one of the most destructive traits of character. Unrestrained anger can lead us to say and do things we later regret, hurt the people we love, damage relationships, and cause lasting emotional and even physical harm.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ repeatedly cautioned against giving in to anger and offered profound advice on how to control it. His teachings remind us that true strength is not in overpowering others, but in having the self-control to restrain ourselves when we are angry.

In today's world, anger can have serious consequences, particularly for young people. When anger becomes difficult to manage, it can interfere with family life, friendships, relationships, and education. It is therefore important to recognize what triggers our anger, understand its effects, and learn how to respond in a way that reflects the character of a believer.

This program is designed for youth, boys and girls, between the ages of 12 and 18. Together, we will explore:

  • What causes us to become angry?
  • How can we recognize and control our anger before it controls us?
  • What guidance did the Prophet ﷺ give us about dealing with anger?
  • How should we respond when someone else is angry?
  • What are the spiritual, emotional, and social consequences of uncontrolled anger?

Join us for an important and practical discussion on developing self-control, strengthening our character, and learning not to let Shaytan take advantage of our moments of anger.

We strongly encourage all youth to attend and benefit from this timely and relevant program.
