About the Evening

A monthly evening for the whole family

Once a month, our community gathers under one roof — parents, children, and elders — for an evening built around remembrance, reflection, and connection.

The night opens with a reminder from Shaykh Zahir Bacchus, designed to renew one's faith and strengthen one's connection with Allah سُبْحَانَهُ وَتَعَالَى. A live Q&A follows, giving everyone — young and old — a chance to ask questions openly.

A warm dinner is served to all attendees, so come hungry and stay for the company.

Monthly Speaker

How the Evening Flows

What to Expect

  • Maghrib
    / Isha     Reminder begins — a talk from Shaykh Zahir Bacchus to renew faith and reflection.
  • ~30 min
    later     Q&A session — open floor for questions from attendees of all ages.
  • To
    close     Dinner is served — a shared meal for the whole family in the basement hall.

Start time shifts with the season — in Spring and Summer, we begin after Maghrib; in Winter, after Isha. Check our prayer time schedule on the day of the event for the exact start.
