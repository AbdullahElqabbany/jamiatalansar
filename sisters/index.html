About the Program

An evening of learning & connection

Join us in an evening of learning, connection, and spiritual growth. Each month, our sisters gather to benefit from a short talk on a relevant Islamic topic, followed by open discussion and time to connect over light refreshments.

Whether you're new to the community or have been with us for years, this is a space built for every sister to feel welcome — to ask questions, share reflections, and grow together in faith.

We look forward to seeing you, insha'Allah!

Program Includes

  • A short talk on a relevant Islamic topic
  • Open discussion
  • Light refreshments
