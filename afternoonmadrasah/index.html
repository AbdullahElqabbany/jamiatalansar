About the Program

Building the next generation on a foundation of Quran and knowledge

The Afternoon Youth Madrasah at Jamiat Ul Ansar is a weekday program running after school, designed to give children aged 6–12 a structured and nurturing environment where they can learn to recite the Quran correctly, understand their deen, and build the habits of a Muslim life — all while staying connected to their community.

What We Cover

A balanced curriculum for the whole child

Correct Pronunciation
Mastering Tajweed — the rules of proper Quranic recitation — so every letter is given its right.
Quran Recitation Fluency
Building confidence and smoothness in reciting the Quran, progressing at each child's pace.
Essential Daily Du'as
Memorising the supplications for every part of the day — waking, eating, travelling, and more.
Seerah of the Prophet ﷺ
Exploring the life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — his character, his struggles, and his mercy.
Fiqh of Daily Life
Islamic rulings on wudu, salah, purity, and the everyday matters children encounter as Muslims.

Your Teacher
Shaykh Zahid Ally
Lead Instructor, Afternoon Madrasah

Shaykh Zahid Ally has been an educator for over 30 years. He spent seven years in Damascus, Syria, studying the Islamic sciences and earning ijazāt (authorizations) to teach Qur’an, Hadith, Fiqh, ‘Aqidah, and other disciplines. He taught at Sakinah Elementary School in Edmonton for more than 15 years and has been offering professional development workshops to principals and school administrators in the public school system for many years. In addition, he has been conducting youth workshops for over 15 years, dedicated to nurturing faith, character, and leadership in young people. Along with him will be other qualified teachers as well
