About the Program

A structured path for young adults completing high school or attending university

Study traditional texts in Fiqh, ʿAqidah, Hadith, Tafsir, and Akhlaq (Character Refinement), with discussion, reflection, and practical application. Develop confidence, clarity, and balance in practicing your faith, and learn principles for navigating today's online religious content and contemporary questions.

Subjects of Study

Five core Islamic sciences

Fiqh
Islamic rulings and their reasoning, applied to everyday life.
ʿAqidah
Core beliefs of Islam, understood clearly and with confidence.
Hadith
The words and guidance of the Prophet ﷺ.
Tafsir
Understanding the meaning and context of the Quran.
Akhlaq (Character Refinement)
Refining character and manners in line with the example of the Prophet ﷺ.
