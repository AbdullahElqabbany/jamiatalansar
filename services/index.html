Our Services

What We Offer

How we serve our community

From spiritual guidance to practical support, Jamiat Ul Ansar is here to help. All services are open to members of our community — please email us to get started.

🤲
Pastoral
Counselling

Shaykh Zahir Bacchus and Ustadha Saiema Din provide family, youth, and religious counselling throughout the week. If you need counselling, please email us.

Get in touch →
💍
Marriage
Nikkah Services

Shaykh Zahir is a registered marriage officer in the province of Ontario and conducts Nikkah ceremonies. Please email us for further information and scheduling.

Get in touch →
⚖️
Dispute Resolution
Divorce

In times of disagreement, our Imams not only facilitate dialogue between both parties when resolution is possible, but also clarify the Islamic jurisprudence involved when it is not.

Get in touch →
🕊️
Bereavement
Funeral Services

The mosque provides space for Janazah prayers and for wakes. Please inquire with the mosque office concerning arrangements, or email us directly.

Get in touch →
🌐
Outreach
Inter-Faith Dialogue

Our Imams regularly meet with representatives of other faiths to discuss cross-religious and cross-cultural issues. Shaykh Zahir and Ustadha Saiema Din also give workshops to local high schools on Islam and Muslims.

Get in touch →
🌙
Support
New Muslim Outreach

A dedicated committee provides support, advice, and education to new Muslims — overseeing outreach to our fellow citizens across the Brampton community. Email us for more details.

Get in touch →
🍱
Community
Food Drive for the Poor

Our monthly "Feed the Poor" program brings volunteers together to cook and distribute 300 meals to four shelters across the GTA. We also regularly collect and distribute non-perishable food. See Br. Peter at the mosque, or email us.
