Spiritual Nourishment

Fifteen minutes that change your day

Every day at our masjid, a small circle gathers for a short and focused reminder rooted in the Sunnah. We read a hadith from Riyad As-Saliheen, the celebrated collection of the Prophet's ﷺ sayings compiled by Imam An-Nawawi, followed by a brief explanation that draws out its meaning for our daily lives.

These gatherings are intentionally short — no more than fifteen minutes — so that anyone, regardless of how busy their day is, can attend and walk away with a piece of timeless wisdom to carry with them.

All are welcome. No registration is needed — simply join us in the 2nd Floor Men's Prayer Hall at the appointed time.

Conducted By

Shaykh Zahir Bacchus

Leading our daily gatherings with a focus on the practical application of hadith in everyday life, helping our community stay rooted in the Sunnah amid the busyness of the day.
